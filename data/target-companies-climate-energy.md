## How this plugs into the pipeline

- **Tier 1 + the ✅ scanner rows in Tier 2** → add to `portals.yml` `tracked_companies` and the existing `scan.mjs` picks them up for free (zero tokens).
- **⚠️ scrape rows (Personio/Teamtailor/Recruitee + all Tier 3 majors)** → targets for the separate browser-scraper service. Personio scaleups rate-limit and have no clean public API; the majors run Avature/SuccessFactors/Workday/Phenom. Note two easier entry points: **Vattenfall** exposes a SmartRecruiters postings API, and **Personio** boards share one predictable HTML shape across all Tier-2 Personio companies (one scraper covers GridX, Entrix, Suena, Zolar, Planted, ecoligo, Envelio, reverion at once).
